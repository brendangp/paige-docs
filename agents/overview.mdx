Paige gives you four AI agents in the chat sidebar. Each is scoped to a part of the dashboard, so the agent you're talking to matches whatever you're doing.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Code Agent" icon="code" href="/agents/code-agent">
    Builds and edits your bot from plain-language instructions. Your main way to change what the bot does.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Conversations Agent" icon="messages-square" href="/agents/conversations-agent">
    Helps you work through your inbox — finding contacts, reading history, and drafting replies for you to send.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Broadcasting Agent" icon="megaphone" href="/agents/broadcasting-agent">
    Builds audiences, drafts templates, and assembles campaigns for you to approve. Never sends on its own.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Help Agent" icon="circle-help" href="/agents/help-agent">
    Answers questions about Paige itself — how features work and where to find things.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  These builder agents are separate from **[the AI in your bot](/guides/ai-in-your-bot)** — the AI that runs inside your deployed bot and talks to your customers on WhatsApp. That one is your own code and bills differently; see [What's billed](#whats-billed) below.
</Note>

## What's billed

All four agents bill against your [credits](/guides/billing#ai-credits), the Help Agent included. Every prompt costs something, and the more work you ask for, the more it uses. A small question costs very little.

| Agent | Cost |
|---|---|
| [Code Agent](/agents/code-agent) | Billed against your credits |
| [Conversations Agent](/agents/conversations-agent) | Billed against your credits |
| [Broadcasting Agent](/agents/broadcasting-agent) | Billed against your credits |
| [Help Agent](/agents/help-agent) | Billed against your credits |

When your balance reaches zero, none of them starts a new request until someone tops up. See [Low-credit warning and zero-balance gating](/guides/billing#low-credit-warning-and-zero-balance-gating).

## Where your spend shows up

**Settings → Usage** shows your balance, about how many prompts it will still buy, and a history with one row per request, however many replies or AI models it took. Tap **parts** on a row that took several replies to see what each one cost. See [The Usage page](/guides/billing#the-usage-page).
