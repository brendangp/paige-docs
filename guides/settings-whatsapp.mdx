The **WhatsApp** section of the [Settings tab](/guides/settings-project) is where a project's number is connected and managed.

- **Not connected yet?** You'll confirm you have a Meta Business account and a number that can receive an OTP, then run Meta's embedded signup. The full step-by-step is on [Connect your WhatsApp number](/whatsapp-setup) — this section links you straight into it rather than repeating it.
- **Already connected?** You'll see your profile photo, phone number, display name, and username, along with your quality rating, throughput, messaging limit, and business verification status — plus a business profile form and a reminder about your Meta payment method.

## What you can manage here

- **Profile** — your profile photo and the business profile form: about, description, address, email, and websites.
- **Display name** — the name customers see in their chat list. Changing it is reviewed by Meta before it takes effect.
- **Username** — claim an `@handle` so customers can find you without knowing your number. See [Claim a WhatsApp username](/whatsapp-setup#claim-a-whatsapp-username).
- **Business details** — the industry category sent to Meta, and the rest of your public business information.
- **Quality rating and messaging limits** — Meta's health signals for your number: your current quality rating, your throughput, and how many unique customers you can start conversations with in a rolling 24 hours.
- **Business verification** — whether Meta has verified your business, and a **Get verified** button when it hasn't. Meta only lets verified businesses publish flows.
- **Payment method** — a reminder, with a **Check payment method** button, that Meta needs a payment method before it can charge for a broadcast.
- **Danger Zone** — **Disconnect** the number so the project can connect a different one. See [Changing your WhatsApp number](/whatsapp-setup#changing-your-whatsapp-number).

## Business verification and your Meta payment method

Two things on Meta's side decide what your number can actually do, and this section is where Paige surfaces both.

- **Business Verification** — Meta confirming your business is real, from documents such as a registration certificate or a utility bill. Meta only lets verified businesses publish [flows](/guides/flows/overview), so an unverified business can build and test flows but can't take them live on its own number. When Meta reports you're not verified, a **Get verified** button appears next to the status and opens Meta Business Manager in a new tab. If Paige can't tell either way, you see no button and no claim that you're unverified.
- **Payment method** — Meta charges for [broadcasts](/guides/broadcasts) and other messages your business starts, and those messages fail to send if no payment method is attached to your business in Meta. A reminder with a **Check payment method** button opens your Meta payment settings. Seeing the reminder doesn't mean anything is wrong: Meta gives Paige no reliable way to check, so the reminder shows either way.

Fixed something in Meta? Click **Recheck** to refresh the status right away instead of waiting a few minutes.

## Changing your display name

Type the new name next to **Display Name** and click **Request Change**.

Paige checks with WhatsApp that the request was actually taken before telling you it worked. If WhatsApp turns it down you get WhatsApp's own reason; and if WhatsApp accepts the request but keeps no record of it, you're told that too — rather than being left waiting for a review that was never started.

Most names go for review and the status shows **Pending review** until WhatsApp decides; some are approved straight away. While a change is being reviewed the status turns **yellow** and a line underneath tells you which name is waiting. **Your current name stays live on WhatsApp until the new one is approved.** The status updates as soon as the request goes through, so you don't need to reload the page.

<Warning>
  WhatsApp allows **10 display-name changes every 30 days**, and a request **cannot be withdrawn** — sending a second name does not cancel the first. Check the spelling before you click.
</Warning>

## Disconnecting your number

The **Danger Zone** at the bottom of the section holds one action: **Disconnect**. It takes the number off the project and deregisters it with Meta, so the project can connect a different one. You need to be the **project owner** or a **workspace admin**.

Click **Disconnect**, type the confirmation phrase in the dialog, then confirm. Your conversations, contacts, messages, and code are kept. Your [scheduled tasks](/guides/scheduled-tasks) are paused until a new number is connected.

<Warning>
  **Save the two-step verification PIN the dialog shows you before you confirm.** Paige emails you a copy, and that's the only time you see it. Without that PIN nobody can re-register the number — WhatsApp has no way to switch two-step verification off automatically, so you'd have to turn it off yourself in WhatsApp Manager (**Account tools → Phone numbers → the gear icon → Turn off two-step verification**).
</Warning>
