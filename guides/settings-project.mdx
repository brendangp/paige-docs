The **Settings** tab is where you manage the project as a whole — as opposed to the [Tools tab](/guides/tools), which holds the infrastructure your bot runs on. Its sections are split across the pages in this group; the **Project** section, covered here, is the tab's home base.

## Project

From the **Project** section you can:

- **Rename the project** — edit the name field; it saves when you click away.
- See the project's **status** (`active`, `draft`) and the date it was **created**.
- Check the **WhatsApp connection** at a glance — it reads *Connected to WhatsApp Business API* or *Not connected*. Manage the connection itself on the [WhatsApp](/guides/settings-whatsapp) page.
- Use the **Share Your WhatsApp** card to hand out a wa.me link or QR code to start a chat with your bot.
- Link a phone for testing on the **Paige Dev** card — see [Testing](/guides/testing).
- Import contacts from the contacts card — see [Contacts import](/guides/contacts-import). Once Broadcasts is on, the same card also shows **Manage contacts →**, which takes you to the full [contact list and tags](/guides/broadcasts#your-contacts-and-their-tags).
- Activate **Broadcasts** for the project. If tagging contacts in bulk ever tells you this project needs an update, come back here and click **Repair broadcasting** — it's a one-off, and sending broadcasts keeps working in the meantime.

<Warning>
  The **Danger Zone** at the bottom has two irreversible actions. **Reset Project** removes all code, conversations, and messages (your contacts, their notes and your WhatsApp connection are kept). **Delete Project** removes everything. Neither can be undone.
</Warning>

### Paige Dev — test on your phone

The **Paige Dev** card lets you link your real phone number and message a shared test number to try your bot on actual WhatsApp, without a number of your own and without touching your live data. Linking, moving a number between projects, the **Try now** test template, and **Clear test data** are all covered in full on the [Testing](/guides/testing) page.

### Conversation access

When several people answer WhatsApp chats on one project, the **Conversation access** card decides who sees what. It appears on projects that belong to a [workspace](/guides/workspaces), and lists each member with one of three levels:

| Level | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Manager** | See every conversation and [assign conversations](/guides/conversations-tab#assigning-conversations) to team members. |
| **All conversations** | See every conversation and who it's assigned to, but not assign. This is where everyone starts. |
| **Only assigned** | See only the conversations assigned to them. |

The workspace **owner** and **admins** are always managers. Making someone an admin of the workspace makes them a manager straight away. Only a manager can change someone's level.

**What "Only assigned" leaves out.** Someone on **Only assigned** can reply, send templates and files, take a conversation over, tag the contact and read and write its notes, but only for their own contacts. On top of that:

- They can't open **Broadcasts**, the **Database**, **Storage**, **Logs** or **Secrets**.
- They can't deploy the bot. There's no **Deploy** button for them.
- In the chat panel they can only use the **Conversations** and **Help** agents. On the Build and Settings tabs, that means Help.

**Live updates and notifications follow the same rule.** Someone on **Only assigned** only sees new messages arrive live, and only gets phone notifications, for their own conversations. When a manager assigns them one, they get a **New conversation assigned** notification. If a manager changes your level while you have Paige open, your tabs and live updates change straight away, with no reload.

If you remove someone from the workspace, or they leave, their conversations go back to being unassigned.

<Note>
  **Access levels apply to people signed in to Paige.** An [API key or a connected AI agent](/guides/settings-api) doesn't belong to a person, so it still sees every conversation.
</Note>

If an **Only assigned** member sees **"Conversation assignment isn't set up for this project yet"**, the project's database hasn't been updated for assignments. Nothing can be shown to them until it is, and in the meantime nobody on that project can be set to **Only assigned**. Everyone else is unaffected.

## Notifications

The **Notifications** section holds a single **Push notifications** switch for the device you're on. Turn it on to get a push when a customer needs you, a deploy finishes, or a broadcast changes state. It's per-device, so switch it on for each phone or computer you want alerts on. See [Notifications](/guides/mobile-app#notifications).

## Numbers

You may see a **Numbers** section just below **WhatsApp**. It is where you rent a WhatsApp-ready South African phone number from Green Pages for R90 a month, and manage the numbers you hold: their subscriptions, payments and invoices. You register a number in the [WhatsApp](/guides/settings-whatsapp#connecting-a-green-pages-number) section. See [Get a WhatsApp number from Green Pages](/guides/numbers).

<Note>
  Numbers is being rolled out gradually. If the section isn't in your Settings, it isn't available on your account yet. Only the project owner or a workspace admin sees it.
</Note>

## Where secrets live

If you came here looking for the API keys and tokens **your bot** uses, they're not on the Settings tab — they moved to **Tools → Secrets**. Managing them is covered on the [Secrets](/guides/secrets) page.

## API and AI agents

**Settings → API** is a separate section, for giving other apps and AI agents programmatic access to the project. It's where you create Paige API keys, register webhooks, and connect an agent like Claude, ChatGPT, or Codex — including attaching several projects to one connection. It's visible to owners and admins only. See [API settings](/guides/settings-api) for the tour, [Connect an AI agent](/guides/connect-an-agent) for the agent route, and the [API reference](/api-reference/introduction) if you're calling Paige from your own code.

<Note>
  On a phone, Settings sits behind the **☰** menu and each section is its own screen. See [Use Paige on your phone](/guides/mobile-app).
</Note>
